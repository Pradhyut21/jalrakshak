# Reuse audit and final implementation choice

Prepared 1 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. This records observed repository licensing and engineering suitability, not a legal clearance of every asset or transitive dependency. Upstream code was read, not executed. No packages were installed and no submission application was created.

## Decision

**Build original, focused JalRakshak domain code after the official kickoff, using mature dependencies normally. Do not fork or rename a complete reference product.** Use FastAPI, React/Vite and AWS SAM for their established infrastructure roles; add the Strands SDK only if the deterministic water/action loop is complete and the assistant earns its place. FastAPI/Vite licensing is recorded separately by the parent audit.

JalRakshak needs measurement-anchored tank accounting, reserve/empty/deadline calculations, point-in-time forecasting, site-scoped access, explicit approval, durable notification reconciliation and fresh-reading verification. Those behaviors differ substantially from AWS outage repair, evacuation drills and merchant fraud review. A complete fork would require removing much of the original product and repairing unsuitable assumptions, while carrying its history and license obligations. The practical speed gain comes from dependencies, a small stable contract, focused tests and a reproducible demo.

WNTR/EPANET, custom deep learning, broad agent harnesses, voice infrastructure and 3D engines are outside the MVP. This is a scope decision, not a claim that those projects lack value.

## What to accept and reject technically

- **Accept as independently implemented design patterns:** one incident timeline; fact/evidence identifiers; explicit action review; separate publication/receipt/repair states; deterministic verification; a labeled scenario/reset route; fixed evaluation artifacts; a real local HTTP receiver test.
- **Reject Beacon consent code as our authorization implementation:** it uses transcript substring matching and its contract read-back does not bind a subsequent turn and exact spoken duration. Our consent must be a structured authenticated decision bound to a proposal version and destination scope. [Consent code](https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77/Beacon/blob/c3f63e53faf07fe5fb5f19255ddb0ddbb7156c27/src/beacon/voice_tools.py#L313-L343)
- **Reject Beacon's telemetry semantics for water:** its error metric can interpret missing recent datapoints as zero errors. Water readings require UNKNOWN when missing or stale. Its simulator also manufactures recovery metrics/OK alarm after the rule is restored; our simulated observation stream must be labeled and must not masquerade as independent physical evidence. [Verification](https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77/Beacon/blob/c3f63e53faf07fe5fb5f19255ddb0ddbb7156c27/src/beacon/remediation/verify.py#L89-L141), [local simulation](https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77/Beacon/blob/c3f63e53faf07fe5fb5f19255ddb0ddbb7156c27/scripts/local_server.py#L194-L268)
- **Reject Campus's complete simulation stack:** 3D first-person controls, physics, two-role movement networking and client-authored drill verification add complexity without improving a caretaker's supply deadline. Its source has no identified reuse grant.
- **Reject Fraud's complete ML/service stack:** financial labels, XGBoost artifacts, fraud graphs and dispute PDFs do not fit water accounting. Its restrictive license independently excludes selected source reuse. Prior static review also found historical-feature timing and notification failure problems; those are lessons to avoid, not implementation foundations.
